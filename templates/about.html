OUR STORY

Why ScamShield AI?

In today's digital world, online scams and fraudulent messages are becoming increasingly common. Many people receive suspicious messages but cannot easily identify whether they are genuine or fraudulent.

ScamShield AI was developed to make scam awareness easier by providing an AI-based message analysis platform that helps users make more informed decisions.
